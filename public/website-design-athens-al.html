CLEAR ANSWERS FOR YOUR NEXT STEP

Before your Athens
website takes shape.

Ask us about your Athens plans

Can a downtown Athens shop get a useful site without a large online store?+

Yes. A small site can show your products or specialties, opening hours, location, and contact details. If you mainly sell in person around the square, we can focus on helping people decide to visit. Online shopping can be discussed separately if it fits your business.

Can the site explain where I take jobs outside Athens?+

We’ll ask for your actual coverage area and any differences in the service you provide. You might travel across parts of Limestone County or stay close to Athens. The wording should tell a customer whether their job is a fit before they spend time requesting a quote.

What should I send if my Athens business has never had a website?+

Start with your business name, contact details, services, and a few examples of the work you do. Photos, a logo, and common customer questions help too. We can discuss the missing pieces and agree on a content plan before starting the design.

Will an Athens redesign keep my existing website address?+

In many cases, yes. We’ll check your domain, hosting, and email setup before deciding how to launch. Keeping a familiar address can matter when Athens customers already have it on a business card, vehicle, or saved link.

Can I share project photos and customer feedback?+

Yes, when you have permission to use them. We can organize completed Athens-area jobs into examples with helpful descriptions. Reviews stay attributed to the original person, and we won’t add a location or result that the source doesn’t confirm.

Do you manage the website after it goes live?+

We can discuss maintenance, hosting, content changes, and ongoing search work. For an Athens business, an update plan might cover new service photos, seasonal information, or changed contact details. The support scope is agreed separately so you know what is included.
